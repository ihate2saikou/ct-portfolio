Reflection on Data Analysis Task

I developed a stronger understanding of how spreadsheets can be used to organise, analyse, and present data effectively. Throughout the task, I learnt how to enter and format data, sort and filter information, and create graphs to clearly display trends. These skills helped me recognise patterns in the dataset and communicate my findings in a logical way.

One challenge I faced was ensuring that my formulas and data were accurate. Even small mistakes could affect the results, so I had to carefully check my work and correct any errors. This taught me the importance of attention to detail and verifying information before drawing conclusions.

Overall, I am pleased with the result of my spreadsheet because it was well organised and presented the Spotify streaming data clearly. If I were to complete the task again, I would spend more time improving the visual design of my charts and exploring additional spreadsheet features to provide better analysis. This task improved both my spreadsheet skills and my confidence in analysing real-world data, which will be valuable in future Computing Technology tasks.
